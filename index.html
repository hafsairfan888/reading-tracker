About

Reading Tracker is a visual reading analysis tool I designed to give readers a clearer picture of what to expect from a book. Using color-coded indicators, it maps elements such as reader engagement, pacing, mood shifts, and optional spice warnings — offering insights that go beyond a simple star rating.

Instead of only answering "Was this book good?", the Reading Tracker answers "What is the reading experience actually like?" It highlights where a story picks up, slows down, or reaches its biggest payoff, making a book's overall flow easier to understand at a glance.

This is especially helpful for books with slow starts that later become favorites. Many readers DNF hidden gems simply because the beginning doesn't reflect the rest of the story. The Reading Tracker shows exactly when a book finds its rhythm, helping readers decide whether it's worth pushing through — and it can just as easily reveal books that start strong but lose momentum later, so readers can choose stories that match their preferred pace.

For readers who prefer to know what they're getting into, optional indicators — like spice warnings — help them prepare for or skip certain sections based on their own comfort level. Rather than replacing traditional reviews, the Reading Tracker complements them, turning the reading experience into an easy-to-understand visual guide.
